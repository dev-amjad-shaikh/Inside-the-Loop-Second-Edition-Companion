# Return policy

Version 1. Applies to orders placed from January 1, 2025 through March 31, 2026.

## Return window

Products may be returned within 30 days of delivery. Start a return from the order page.

## Opened products

Opened products may be returned within 30 days of delivery, less a 15% restocking fee. Defective products are exempt from the fee.

## Refunds

Approved refunds are issued to the original payment method within 10 business days.
