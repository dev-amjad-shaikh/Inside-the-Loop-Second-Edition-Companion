# Return policy

Version 2. Applies to orders placed on or after April 1, 2026.

## Return window

Unopened products may be returned within 30 days of delivery for a full refund. Start a return from the order page or contact support with the order number.

## Opened products

Opened products cannot be returned. This includes items whose seal, box or protective film has been removed, and items that have been assembled, installed or used.

The following conditions are exempt from this rule:

| Condition | Returnable | Window |
|---|---|---|
| Defective or damaged on arrival | Yes | 90 days from delivery |
| Missing parts or accessories | Yes | 30 days from delivery |
| Wrong item shipped | Yes | 30 days from delivery |

## Refunds

Approved refunds are issued to the original payment method within 5 business days of the return being received.
